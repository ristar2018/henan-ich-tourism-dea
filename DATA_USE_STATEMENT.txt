DATA USE STATEMENT
==================
Project: From Heritage Resources to Tourism Outcomes — Spatial Efficiency
Analysis of Intangible Cultural Heritage–Tourism Conversion in Henan
Province, China.
Study year: 2024 (cross-section); units: 18 prefecture-level divisions of
Henan Province.

1. WHAT IS RELEASED
-------------------
Only municipality-level aggregates for 18 administrative units are released:
  - counts of national- and provincial-list intangible cultural heritage (ICH)
    items, provincial representative inheritors, traditional villages and
    national key cultural-relic protection units;
  - municipality-level economic, demographic and geographic variables
    (GDP, permanent population, urbanization rate, tertiary-sector share,
    mean elevation, administrative area);
  - total tourism revenue and the official end-2024 count of all-grade
    A-level scenic areas, with a city-by-city source register;
  - derived composite/density indices and the efficiency results produced by
    the released scripts.
All point-level or individual-level information has been aggregated to the
municipal level.

2. WHAT IS NOT RELEASED AND WHY
-------------------------------
  - Individual ICH item coordinates and site names, inheritor-level data and
    the raw IhChina point vector file. The point records are a licensed
    third-party academic product (IhChina 2006-2021) whose redistribution is
    restricted by the vendor licence, and site-level coordinates could
    re-identify individual heritage resources. Researchers who hold the
    appropriate licence can reconstruct the released municipal counts by the
    spatial-join procedure documented in spatial_reconstruction/README.md.
  - Administrative boundary polygons and the raw elevation raster. These are
    third-party/official products and are not redistributed; only the
    municipality-level area and zonal-mean elevation derived from them are
    released.

3. SOURCES OF THE RELEASED STATISTICS
-------------------------------------
  - Municipal 2024 national economic and social development statistical
    communiques and the corresponding statistical yearbooks (GDP, population,
    urbanization, tourism revenue where published).
  - Municipal culture-and-tourism authority releases and graded scenic-area
    rosters (A-level scenic areas). Four municipalities (Kaifeng, Anyang,
    Xinxiang and Sanmenxia) publish only a grade subtotal or a related-year
    figure in their communique; their all-grade total is taken from the
    municipal authority. Zhengzhou's end-2024 count is taken from the
    municipal government's 2025 official release. All 18 A-level figures are
    official; none is estimated by the authors.
  - National and provincial ICH lists, the Chinese traditional-village lists
    and national key cultural-relic protection unit lists (publicly
    announced counts).
  - A public digital-elevation model (SRTM/CASEarth family) for mean
    elevation.
Where a 2024 figure was not directly reported, adjacent-year official values
were used under documented reconstruction rules; the affected cells are
flagged in the status columns and listed in Supplementary Table S3 of the
manuscript and in docs/data_dictionary.md.

5. ACCURACY AND USE
-------------------
The data are provided for academic research and teaching, in the form
verified during manuscript revision. Reconstructed/back-cast cells are
explicitly flagged and their sensitivity is assessed in the manuscript and in
scripts r7 (revenue perturbation) and r8 (Simar–Wilson bootstrap). No
warranty is made for decisions taken outside this research context.
